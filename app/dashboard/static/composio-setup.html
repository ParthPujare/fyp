Connect your apps

Three steps. No technical knowledge needed.

Step 1 Paste your key

  1. Open your integration provider's dashboard at app.composio.dev and copy your API key.
  2. In the dashboard go to Account → Integrations → Step 1, paste it, and press Save & check key.
  3. It tells you straight away whether the key works. App logins are handled for you.

Step 2 Connect

  1. Under Step 2, find your app (Gmail, GitHub, Slack…). Use the search box if needed.
  2. Press Connect. A login window opens. Sign in to that app.
  3. App not listed? Ask an admin to add it under Admin — add app for everyone.

Step 3 Refresh, then chat

  1. Back in the dashboard, press Refresh next to the app. It turns active.
  2. Open Chat and ask, for example, check my mail.
  3. If the assistant needs an app you have not connected, it shows a Connect button in the chat. Press it, Refresh, then send again.

If something looks wrong

  • Key rejected: copy it again from the provider's dashboard. Extra spaces break it.
  • Still pending: finish the login window completely, then press Refresh again.
  • App missing: an admin adds it once in the app catalog, and everyone gets it.
  • Sending or changing something asks for approval: that is normal. An approver decides it in Approvals.
